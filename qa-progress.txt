### TC-012 [FAIL] Admin cancel session — blocked when participants are enrolled

  - **Error banner locator**: when the error message appears, the page renders `<p class="...text-red-700">{errorMessage}</p>` — use `page.locator('p').filter({ hasText: 'enrolled participants' })`.
